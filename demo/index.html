Houselights

A Gemini agent, built with Google's Agent Development Kit, that reads every seat of a theatrical engagement out of ClickHouse and tells the film programmer what to do about it.

September 2026. I tried to get seven friends into good rows for The Odyssey on IMAX 70mm in Toronto. The seat maps said no. The same data says a lot more to the exhibitor.

Gemini 3.6 Flash · Google ADK (LlmAgent + SequentialAgent) · ClickHouse (MergeTree, materialized view, array functions) · 8 houses · 5,033 sessions · 50,235 seat states
